# Special shop art: prompts (copy-paste)

Card-only items for the Special shop (owner 2026-10-31). Each one must be NEW art: anything that can
also be bought with nyang can't be sold for money. Send each picture back with its name in bold
below; it is cut, saved and wired to a product (`cash_products`, `cash_only` on the item).

To sell by December, the Christmas pack art is needed by mid-November.

---

## A. Christmas Pack ($6.99, Dec 1 – Dec 31)

### A1. Room theme · **christmas-room**

Attach `korehan/assets/room/themes/winter.webp`, then paste:

```
Draw a NEW room background in exactly the same style, size (1000x900), camera angle and layout as the attached picture: same wall height, same floor area, same window positions and sizes, same soft pixel-art painting style.
Theme: a cozy Christmas living room at night. Deep red wallpaper with a faint gold snowflake pattern, a green pine garland with small warm fairy lights along the top of the wall, warm honey-coloured wooden floor with a round cream knitted rug in the middle.
Through the windows: a snowy night town with glowing windows and coloured Christmas lights on the rooftops, snow falling.
Keep the middle of the floor and the lower wall EMPTY (furniture is placed there later). No furniture, no tree, no people, no text.
```

### A2. Outfit · **reindeer**

Attach `avatar-template-4.png` (the four base characters), then paste:

```
Edit the attached picture. Keep the four characters EXACTLY as they are: same faces, same hair, same head size, same body and leg length, same pose, same position in the picture, same soft pixel-art style, plain pure white background. Change ONLY their clothes. New outfit for all four: a cozy brown reindeer onesie with a cream belly patch, a small red pom-pom on the chest and little brown hoof-style slippers. The hood with antlers hangs DOWN on the back (not on the head).
```

### A3. Props (one sheet) · **xmas-fireplace**, **gingerbread-house**, **reindeer-plush**

Paste the room-item style line first (from `room-art-prompts.md`), then:

```
Make ONE wide image: three separate items in a row, evenly spaced, each with plenty of empty white space around it, nothing touching, no labels.
1: a red-brick fireplace with a crackling fire, a pine garland on the mantel and three red-and-white knitted stockings hanging from it.
2: a gingerbread house with white icing on the roof, candy-cane posts and gumdrop decorations, on a small plate.
3: a soft brown reindeer plush toy with felt antlers, a red nose and a tiny green scarf, sitting.
```
