Questions, answered.

I'm at zero Korean — can I really start here?

Yes. Hangul Builder teaches you to read 자모 (the alphabet) from scratch, then Seed-level articles use only the most common patterns. The tap-to-analyze panel means you never have to guess what a word means.

Do I need to pay to use it?

No. Reading articles, stories, conversations, saving vocabulary, building your Universe, daily Sprout writing, Hangul Builder, slang FAST TRACK — all free, forever. Premium adds unlimited AI analysis and live tutoring on top.

Will this help me pass TOPIK?

Indirectly — every article and conversation is tagged by TOPIK level, so you can stay in your zone or push up. KoreHani isn't TOPIK prep specifically; it's daily comprehension practice that makes the test easier when you sit it.

Where does my data live?

Your reading history, saved words, and progress are stored in Supabase (servers in Seoul). We never sell or share user data with third parties. Full privacy policy below.

Can I cancel premium anytime?

Yes — one click in your profile. Free features keep working forever after cancellation. You don't lose your Universe.
