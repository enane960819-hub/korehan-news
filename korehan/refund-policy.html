Refund Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

At a Glance

The short version: cancel anytime, monthly subscriptions are non-refundable once charged, annual subscriptions are pro-rated within the first 30 days. Full details below.

Monthly

Non-refundable once a billing period starts. You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the period.

Annual / 6-month

Pro-rated refund for the unused months if you cancel within the first 30 days of purchase or renewal.

1. Monthly Subscriptions

Monthly subscriptions are generally non-refundable once a billing period has commenced. However, if you experience a technical issue caused by KoreHani that substantially prevents you from using the Service, you may request a pro-rated refund for the unused portion of the current billing period.

You can cancel a monthly subscription at any time from your account page. Cancellation stops the next renewal — you keep access until the end of the current billing period.

2. 6-Month and Annual Subscriptions

For longer subscriptions, you may request a pro-rated refund for the remaining full months of your subscription period if you cancel within the first 30 days of purchase or renewal. After 30 days, longer subscriptions are non-refundable, but you can still cancel auto-renewal at any time.

Pro-rating example: if you purchased an annual plan and cancel after 25 days, you receive a refund for 11 of the 12 months (the current month is consumed).

3. How to Request a Refund

Email hello@korehani.com within the applicable refund window with:

  • Your account email address
  • Date of the charge you want refunded
  • Reason for the refund request

We will review your request and respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically appear in 5–10 business days depending on your card issuer.

4. Exceptions

Refunds will not be issued for:
  • Accounts suspended or terminated for violation of the Terms of Service
  • Free trial periods (no charge has occurred)
  • Charges disputed more than 90 days after the original transaction date
  • Lost progress, missed assignments, or other usage-based claims that don't reflect a Service defect

5. Free Trial

Where a free trial is offered, no charge is made during the trial period. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, you will be automatically charged for the first billing period. You can cancel a free trial at any time through your account settings — no questions asked.

6. Korean Consumer Protection

For Korean residents: this policy is provided in accordance with the Korean Act on the Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce (전자상거래 등에서의 소비자보호에 관한 법률). Where this policy conflicts with non-waivable consumer rights granted by Korean law, those rights apply.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this refund policy from time to time. Material changes apply only to subscriptions started or renewed after the change takes effect — your existing subscription is governed by the policy in effect at the time of your most recent charge.
