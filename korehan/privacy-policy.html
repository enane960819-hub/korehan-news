KoreHani ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services at korehannews.com. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of our site.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account registration: When you sign up, we collect your email address and, if you use Google OAuth, your Google profile name and avatar.
  • Payment information: If you subscribe to a paid plan, billing and payment data is processed by our third-party payment processor (Stripe). We do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Communications: If you contact us via email or support, we retain that correspondence.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Articles read, vocabulary saved, quiz results, study streaks, TOPIK level progress, and other learning activity.
  • Device & technical data: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, referring URL, and time zone.
  • Log data: Server logs recording requests made to our infrastructure (Supabase/Netlify), including timestamps and error codes.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: See Section 3 for details.

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Google OAuth: When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, display name, and profile photo from Google.
  • Supabase: Our database and authentication provider may supply session metadata.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Providing the service: Delivering personalised Korean learning content, tracking progress across five learning axes (Reading, Vocabulary, Grammar, Writing, Listening), and enabling account access.
  • Daily vocabulary assignments: Selecting and delivering 20 Korean words per day based on your TOPIK level and learning history.
  • AI-powered features: Generating vocabulary analysis, grammar breakdowns, and reading missions for articles using the Anthropic Claude API. Your content interactions are used as prompts but are not used to train the underlying AI model.
  • Payment processing: Managing subscriptions, billing, and refunds.
  • Communications: Sending transactional emails (account confirmation, password reset, billing receipts). We do not send marketing emails without your explicit consent.
  • Analytics & improvement: Understanding aggregate usage patterns to improve content and features. We use anonymised or aggregated data where possible.
  • Security & fraud prevention: Detecting and preventing unauthorised access, abuse, or fraudulent activity.
  • Legal compliance: Meeting our obligations under applicable law.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use the following categories of cookies and local storage:

Essential Cookies

Required for the service to function. These include authentication session tokens (managed by Supabase Auth) and CSRF protection tokens. You cannot opt out of these without discontinuing use of the service.

Preference Cookies

Store your settings such as dark/neon display mode, TOPIK level preference, and daily study goals. These are stored in your browser's localStorage and do not leave your device.

Analytics

We may use lightweight, privacy-respecting analytics to measure aggregate page visits and feature usage. We do not use Google Analytics. If third-party analytics are introduced, this policy will be updated and users notified.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies will prevent login and core functionality. Clearing localStorage will reset your local preferences but will not delete account data stored in our database.

4. AI-Generated Content

KoreHani uses Anthropic's Claude AI to generate educational features including vocabulary lists, grammar explanations, and reading comprehension missions associated with news articles.

  • Data sent to AI: Article text and your content interactions (e.g., the article you are reading) may be sent to the Anthropic API to generate contextualised learning content. This is proxied through our own server and your identity is not disclosed to Anthropic beyond what is contained in the prompt.
  • Not used for training: Pursuant to our agreement with Anthropic, data sent through the API is not used to train Anthropic's models.
  • Accuracy disclaimer: AI-generated vocabulary definitions, grammar explanations, and translations are for educational purposes. While we strive for accuracy, KoreHani does not guarantee that AI-generated content is error-free. Users should consult authoritative sources for critical language study.
  • Content cache: AI-generated results are cached in our database to avoid redundant API calls and to improve load times. Cached content may be served to multiple users reading the same article.

5. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

Service Providers

  • Supabase: Database, authentication, and edge function hosting (servers located in the EU/US depending on region). Supabase acts as a data processor under a Data Processing Agreement.
  • Netlify: Static file hosting and CDN delivery. Netlify may log IP addresses for security purposes.
  • Anthropic: AI language model API. Article content is sent to generate learning features as described in Section 4.
  • Stripe (if applicable): Payment processing. Stripe is PCI-DSS compliant.
  • Google: OAuth authentication provider when you choose to sign in with Google.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or government regulation, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, prevent fraud, or protect the safety of our users.

Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email and/or a prominent notice on our site before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

6. Data Retention

  • Active accounts: We retain your personal data and learning history for as long as your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts: When you delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or financial compliance (e.g., payment records retained for 7 years).
  • Server logs: Technical access logs are retained for up to 90 days for security purposes.
  • AI cache: Cached AI-generated content associated with articles may persist indefinitely as it is not personally identifiable.

7. Your Rights Under GDPR (EU/EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of access: You may request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten"): You may request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Right to restriction of processing: You may request that we limit how we process your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to data portability: You may request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: You may object to processing based on our legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You have the right to file a complaint with your national data protection authority.

Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contract performance: Processing necessary to provide the service you have contracted for (account management, delivering learning content).
  • Legitimate interests: Security monitoring, fraud prevention, and service improvement.
  • Consent: Marketing communications, where applicable.
  • Legal obligation: Financial record keeping and compliance with applicable law.

To exercise any GDPR rights, please contact us at privacy@korehannews.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA) grant you the following rights:

  • Right to know: You may request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the categories of sources, and the business purposes for collection.
  • Right to delete: You may request deletion of personal information we have collected, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to correct: You may request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing: We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information: We do not collect sensitive personal information as defined under CPRA beyond what is necessary to provide our service.
  • Right to non-discrimination: We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA/CPRA rights.

Categories of Personal Information Collected (Past 12 Months)

  • Identifiers (email address, IP address, user ID)
  • Internet or electronic network activity (pages visited, features used, reading history)
  • Geolocation (country/timezone, derived from IP — not precise GPS location)
  • Inferences drawn from the above (TOPIK level estimate, learning preferences)

To exercise your CCPA/CPRA rights, contact us at privacy@korehannews.com or use the subject line "California Privacy Request."

9. Children's Privacy

KoreHani is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under these ages. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately at privacy@korehannews.com and we will delete it promptly.

10. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data:

  • All data transmission is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • Authentication is managed by Supabase Auth with support for OAuth 2.0 and PKCE flow.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensure users can only access their own data in the database.
  • The Anthropic API key and Supabase service role key are stored server-side only and never exposed to the client.
  • HTTP security headers are enforced: X-Frame-Options: DENY, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, and a restrictive Permissions-Policy.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we use commercially reasonable measures to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. In the event of a data breach affecting your rights, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.
