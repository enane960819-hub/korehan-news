How Hangul works

Every syllable is a little block

Korean letters stack into blocks: a consonant, a vowel, and sometimes a final consonant underneath. Learn 24 basic letters and you can sound out any word.

  • Pick letters and tap the block to hear it.
  • ㅇ is silent at the start of a block (아) and "ng" at the end (강).
  • Then check yourself with the 10-letter Hangul quiz in the Word Bank.

Questions

Before you start

Which level should I pick?

If a Sprout article feels slow but you understand most sentences, move up. You can switch levels any time — in the Study Room and on the article list.

How much time do I need?

Ten minutes a day is enough. Coming back every day matters more than long sessions — your streak counts any day you review, read or do a lesson step.

Do I need an account?

You can read without one. A free account saves your words, your streak and your progress across devices.

Is there audio?

Yes — every article, word and conversation line has a listen button.
