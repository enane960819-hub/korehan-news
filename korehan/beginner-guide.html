Four steps from passive reading to active Korean

Every article opens into a structured 4-step flow. You don't just read — you build the words, recognize the phrases, fill in the gaps, and prove it on a quiz. Writing lives in its own home (Express Practice) so you can pick a daily topic, free-write, or journal whenever you're ready.

  • 1. Vocab — image-anchored word study with audio and translations
  • 2. Phrases — recognize the natural chunks Koreans actually use
  • 3. Reading — fill blanks back into the article from the word bank
  • 4. Quiz — multiple-choice or type-the-answer, both modes
  • Express Practice — Topic / Free Writing / Diary modes; AI scores instantly, then a real KoreHan tutor reviews every submission before it lands in Growth Lab

Watch every saved word become a star

Growth Lab is more than a stats page. Your Vocabulary Universe turns saved words into a constellation you can explore, while XP trends, streak counts, and a Monthly Report email keep you honest about consistency.
