What beginners should focus on first

If everything feels new, keep your attention on comprehension, repetition, and small outputs instead of perfection.

During reading

  • Understand the topic before you worry about every unknown word.
  • Notice repeated particles, endings, and sentence frames.
  • Save only a small number of words you will actually review.

During review

  • Say example sentences out loud once or twice.
  • Compare similar grammar patterns instead of memorizing isolated rules.
  • Use Study Room soon after reading so the article is still familiar.

During practice

  • Write one simple sentence about what you read.
  • Repeat a short line from Conversations using your own word choice.
  • Track consistency in Learning Hub rather than chasing long sessions.
