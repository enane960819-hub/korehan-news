FAQ
Quick Answers
The questions everyone asks before subscribing.
Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account page in one click. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

What's your refund policy?

Annual plans are pro-rated for unused months within the first 30 days. Monthly plans are non-refundable but you can cancel before the next charge. Full details in the Refund Policy.

Is there a free trial?

No. Every free feature stays free for as long as you like, so you can see how KoreHani works before paying. Premium is billed monthly or yearly, and you can cancel anytime from your account.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your saved words, study history, and Growth Lab stats stay in your account. You can export or delete everything anytime from My Page.

Can I switch between monthly and annual?

Yes, anytime from Manage subscription. Changes are pro-rated by Stripe. Premium Live is monthly only.

What is Premium Live?

Everything in Premium, plus one live class every week with your seat included — Sunday 21:00 KST on Zoom, 60 minutes, 8 learners per class. $27.99/month, monthly only. Reserve or cancel your seat on the Live Class tab until booking closes 1 hour before class. A missed class doesn’t roll over to the next week, and classes are not recorded.

I have Premium. Can I switch to Premium Live?

Yes. Tap Get Premium Live — your subscription switches right away and Stripe credits the unused part of your current plan. You can also switch back from Manage subscription.

How does the writing feedback work?

Weekly writing is part of Premium. It's AI-graded the moment you submit — grammar, vocab, structure and naturalness, with line-by-line corrections — and a KoreHan tutor also reviews it and adds personal notes.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. KoreHani is mobile-first — designed for studying on a phone during commutes or breaks. Works in any modern browser.

Do I need to know Korean already?

No. Content is leveled from Seed (TOPIK 0) through Forest (TOPIK 5-6). Pick your level during onboarding and adjust anytime.

Ready to actually write Korean?

Start free, upgrade when you're ready. Monthly or annual billing, cancel anytime.
