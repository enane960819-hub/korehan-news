FAQ
Quick Answers
The questions everyone asks before subscribing.
Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account page in one click. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

What's your refund policy?

Annual and 6-month plans are pro-rated for unused months within the first 30 days. Monthly plans are non-refundable but you can cancel before the next charge. Full details in the Refund Policy.

Is there a free trial?

The Free tier gives you ongoing access to read articles, conversations, and stories. Subscribe whenever you're ready for daily writing feedback and the full study program.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

Your saved words, study history, and Growth Lab stats stay in your account. You can export or delete everything anytime from My Page.

Can I switch between Standard and Pro?

Yes, anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately (pro-rated). Downgrades apply from the next billing period.

How does the writing feedback work?

Daily Topic Yum Yum in the Study Room is AI-graded the moment you submit — grammar, vocab, structure, and naturalness, with line-by-line corrections and explanations. No waiting.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. KoreHani is mobile-first — designed for studying on a phone during commutes or breaks. Works in any modern browser.

Do I need to know Korean already?

No. Content is leveled from Seed (TOPIK 0) through Forest (TOPIK 5-6). Pick your level during onboarding and adjust anytime.
